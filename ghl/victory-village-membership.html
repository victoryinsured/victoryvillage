Membership Questions

Before You Join.

Is there a contract?
No. Both plans are month to month and renew automatically each month until you cancel.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from the subscription management portal. Canceling stops your next renewal; it doesn’t refund the current billing period. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
What’s the difference between the Toolkit and Pro?
The Toolkit ($47/mo) is the job-hunting toolkit: the AI Victory Coach, job matching and market insights, an application tracker, career tools and the resource library. Pro ($97/mo) adds the private Victory Society community, live workshops and co-working, plus deeper AI analysis of your resume, profile, skill gaps and job matches.
Can I start with the Toolkit and upgrade later?
Yes. Start with the Toolkit and move up to Pro whenever you’re ready. Contact support and we’ll switch your plan.
What happens in a 1:1 Strategy Session?
It’s a private session with Victoria focused on one career decision or problem. Choose a Golden Nugget, Career Lane, Job Placement Strategy or Positioning Session. Sessions are $100 each and only 10 are open each quarter.
Do I need a membership to book a Strategy Session?
Strategy Sessions are an add-on for Toolkit and Pro members, so pick a plan first and then claim your session.
How do I log in after I join?
Check your email for your login details, then sign in to the Student Portal with the email you used at checkout.
