Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to our most common questions.

View All FAQs →
How do I access my courses?
Log in to the Student Portal with the email you used at checkout. Your courses and membership content are listed on your dashboard.
How do I join the community?
The private Victory Village community, live workshops and co-working sessions are part of Victory Village Pro ($97/mo). Choose Pro on our pricing section. After checkout you’ll get an email with your login details and a link to the community. Already on the Toolkit? You can upgrade anytime.
How do I get my certificate?
Once you complete every lesson in a course that includes a certificate, it becomes available in the Student Portal. Can’t find it? Open a support ticket and we’ll help.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes. You can cancel anytime from the subscription management portal. Canceling stops your next renewal; it doesn’t refund the current billing period. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
How do I apply for jobs on the job board?
Open the Job Board inside your membership, pick a role, and use its Apply link to apply directly with the employer. Your career assessment shows which roles fit your background best.
I’m having trouble logging in. What do I do?
Use “Forgot password” on the Student Portal login page and check your spam folder for the reset email. Still stuck? Use Live Chat during business hours for the fastest help.
How do I get feedback on my resume?
Every member gets AI-powered feedback from the AI Victory Coach. Victory Village Pro adds in-depth resume & profile analysis and a skill-gap review. For a personal look, book a 1:1 Strategy Session with Victoria (limited to 10 per quarter).
What’s included in each membership level?
Victory Toolkit ($47/mo) is the job-hunting toolkit: the AI Victory Coach, job matching and market insights, an application tracker, career tools and the resource library. Victory Village Pro ($97/mo) adds the private community, live workshops and co-working, plus deeper AI analysis, resume/profile and skill-gap analysis, and job-match recommendations. The 1:1 Strategy Session ($100) is an add-on with Victoria, limited to 10 per quarter. See the full comparison on our pricing section.
